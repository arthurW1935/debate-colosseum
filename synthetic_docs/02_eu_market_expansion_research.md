*Prepared by: Priya Nair (Head of Growth) & Rahul Sinha (CFO)*

## 1. Why Consider EU Now?

Three converging factors make an EU pilot timely in FY26:

1. **Indian D2C skincare has global credibility:** The Ordinary (Canadian, minimal formulas) proved that ingredient-led skincare transcends geography. NovaSkin's ingredient-transparency positioning is structurally similar and has tested well in early qualitative interviews with UK-based Indian diaspora shoppers.

2. **Series B investor interest:** Both prospective Series B investors have asked specifically about international expansion as part of the growth narrative. A credible EU roadmap strengthens the FY27 revenue story.

3. **Existing organic demand signal:** NovaSkin's website analytics show 2,300–2,800 monthly visitors from UK, Germany, and UAE despite no marketing spend in those geographies. 140 orders have shipped internationally via a temporary "ship worldwide" option in Q4 FY24, with 0 returns.

## 2. Target Markets — Prioritization

### Tier 1 (Pilot Focus): United Kingdom & Germany

**United Kingdom**
- Market size: £3.2B skincare market (2024); growing at ~6.8% CAGR
- Key dynamic: Post-Brexit, the UK has its own cosmetics regulatory framework (UK CPSR) separate from EU CPNP. Brands need a Responsible Person (RP) based in UK.
- Relevant consumer segment: 1.8M Indian diaspora (large, culturally familiar with NovaSkin formulations); millennial skincare enthusiasts on TikTok UK and Instagram
- Competitive set: The Ordinary, CeraVe, Paula's Choice, Medik8 — all much larger but leave room for a value-premium ingredient-led brand
- Amazon UK penetration: High; ASIN creation for EU catalog is straightforward

**Germany**
- Market size: €4.1B skincare market; growing at ~5.2% CAGR
- Regulatory: EU CPNP (Cosmetic Products Notification Portal) required; Responsible Person must be EU-based
- Consumer behavior: German consumers are highly ingredient-literate and value peer review and clinical claims — aligns with NovaSkin's positioning
- E-commerce: Amazon.de is dominant; dm and Rossmann are key offline chains (not relevant for MVP pilot)
- Language: Requires German-language product pages and labels; translation cost estimated at INR 3.5L for full current catalog

### Tier 2 (FY27 if pilot succeeds): UAE, Singapore, Australia

These markets offer India-facing diaspora demand and favorable import duty structures. Excluded from the current pilot scope.

## 3. Regulatory Requirements

### UK Cosmetics Regulatory Framework (Post-Brexit)

| Requirement | Details | Estimated Cost (INR) |
|-------------|---------|----------------------|
| UK Responsible Person | UK-based legal entity or agent | INR 60,000–90,000/year (agent fee) |
| UK CPSR (Cosmetic Product Safety Report) | Required per SKU; written by qualified assessor | INR 18,000–25,000 per SKU |
| Product notification (SCPN) | Online submission per SKU | Minimal (portal fee) |
| Label compliance | UK English, RP address, weight in metric | INR 2L (design + reprint) |
| **Total for 10 SKU pilot, UK** | | **~INR 45L** |

### EU Cosmetics Regulatory Framework (CPNP)

| Requirement | Details | Estimated Cost (INR) |
|-------------|---------|----------------------|
| EU Responsible Person | EU-based entity (can use regulatory agent) | INR 70,000–1,10,000/year |
| CPNP notification | Per SKU, requires Product Information File (PIF) | INR 22,000–32,000 per SKU |
| SCCS dossier (if novel ingredients) | Only required for ingredients on restricted list | INR 2–8L per ingredient if needed |
| German-language label | Per SKU | INR 8,000–12,000 per SKU |
| **Total for 10 SKU pilot, Germany** | | **~INR 52L** |

**Key regulatory risk:** NovaSkin's AHA/BHA Peeling Solution contains 30% AHA — above the EU's over-the-counter limit of 10% at pH < 3.5. This SKU cannot be sold in EU/UK without formula modification or reclassification. Recommend excluding from pilot SKU list.

## 4. Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1 — Soft Launch (Months 1–4)
- Launch on Amazon UK and Amazon.de with optimized listings in local languages
- Use existing inventory buffer, ship from India initially (higher shipping cost, acceptable for validation)
- Target: 200–300 orders/month in combined UK + DE
- Influencer seeding: 10–15 micro-influencers (50K–200K followers) in UK skincare niche; target Indian diaspora and "skintellectual" communities

### Phase 2 — Localization (Months 5–9)
- Move to EU-based 3PL warehouse (Netherlands or Germany)
- Launch novaskin.co website with localized UK landing pages
- Begin paid media on Meta EU (Instagram, Facebook) — €5,000/month starting budget
- Target: 600–800 orders/month combined

### Phase 3 — Scale (Months 10–18)
- Evaluate own-website vs. marketplace revenue split; shift marketing spend toward own site for better margins
- Assess feasibility of adding UAE as a third market
- Target: 1,200–1,500 orders/month combined; reach EU revenue of INR 6.2 Cr/year (break-even)

## 5. Competitive Analysis — EU Skincare Market

| Brand | Positioning | Price | EU Presence |
|-------|-------------|-------|-------------|
| The Ordinary | Ingredient transparency, ultra-low price | £3–£15 | Strong (DECIEM-owned, retail + online) |
| CeraVe | Dermatologist-recommended, ceramides | £8–£20 | Very strong (L'Oréal) |
| Paula's Choice | Science-backed, ingredient-detailed | £20–£65 | Growing |
| Medik8 | Professional-grade, clinical | £25–£80 | UK strong |
| **NovaSkin (proposed)** | **Ingredient-led, mid-premium** | **£8–£28** | **Zero (pilot target)** |

**Key insight:** The £8–£28 price range is competitive but not commoditized in EU. The Ordinary occupies sub-£15 aggressively; NovaSkin would sit above them with more formulation sophistication. CeraVe owns the dermatologist mass channel. Paula's Choice is the closest positioning analog but targets older, higher-income consumers.

## 6. Operational Considerations

### Logistics

**Current state:** NovaSkin fulfills all orders from Bhiwandi warehouse (Shadowfax). International shipping from India to UK is GBP 6–9 per order (courier, 5–8 business days). Acceptable for testing, unacceptable at scale.

**EU warehouse options evaluated:**
- **Own stock in Netherlands (ShipBob EU):** €0.25/unit storage + €3.20 fulfillment per order. Requires minimum 500 units per SKU per quarter. ✓ Feasible
- **Amazon FBA EU:** 15% referral fee + fulfillment fee. Higher cost but zero logistics headache. ✓ Preferred for pilot Phase 1.
- **Partnered UK 3PL (Huboo):** Mid-cost, flexible. ✓ Evaluate for Phase 2 own-website orders.

### Customs & Duties
- India-UK: 12% customs duty on cosmetics (HS code 3304). Factored into pricing model; NovaSkin prices are still competitive after duty.
- India-EU: 6.5% customs duty + 19% VAT (Germany). EU pricing will be set inclusive of all applicable taxes.

### Payment & Currency
- Stripe supports EUR and GBP on Shopify Plus with no additional setup
- FX risk: Revenue in EUR/GBP, costs in INR — natural hedge to an extent. Formal hedging recommended once quarterly EU revenue exceeds INR 50L.

## 9. Decision Recommendation (Growth Team)

The Growth team recommends **proceeding with a phased EU pilot in FY26**, contingent on:
1. Series B close by Sep 2025 (provides the INR 1.85 Cr one-time setup capital)
2. Regulatory filing initiated by May 2025 to hit a Jan 2026 launch window
3. Approval of a dedicated EU Marketing Manager hire (included in FY26 headcount plan)

The risk of not expanding is arguably as high as the execution risk: NovaSkin's Series B valuation story depends on a credible international expansion narrative, and two pilot years of real EU revenue data would be transformative for a Series C or acquisition conversation in FY28.
