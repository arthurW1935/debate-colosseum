## 4. Strategic Discussion: Three Open Decisions

The Board spent the majority of the meeting on three open strategic questions submitted by the management team:

---

### Decision A: EU Market Expansion

**Aryan Mehta:** Summarized the EU expansion research (full report circulated Jan 15). Recommendation from management team is to proceed with a pilot in FY26 contingent on Series B close.

**Pramod Nair (Fireside):** "I'm broadly supportive. The organic traffic from UK/Germany without any marketing is a meaningful signal. The question is sequencing — should EU expansion compete for the Series B capital, or should it wait until NovaSkin is generating more surplus cash domestically?"

**Sucharita Bose (Blume):** "I'd push management to think about this differently. The Series B narrative benefits enormously from a credible international story. The question isn't 'can we afford EU' — it's 'can we afford to not have an EU story when Investor A is doing diligence?' I think a small pilot, under INR 2 Cr in year one, is cheap at the price relative to what it does for the fundraising story."

**Dr. Kavya Agarwal (Independent Director):** "From a risk perspective, my concern is regulatory. The EU cosmetics framework is genuinely demanding, and I've seen smaller brands get burned by assuming compliance was straightforward. I'd want a regulatory consultant engaged before any commitment to a launch date."

**CEO's response:** Confirmed regulatory consultant would be engaged by May 2025 as a precondition. The go-no-go on EU is formally after Series B close.

**Board consensus:** EU expansion is directionally approved as a FY26 initiative, contingent on: (1) Series B close, (2) regulatory filing completion, and (3) a lean budget under INR 2 Cr for year one setup. **Not a formal board resolution — a directional steer.**

---

### Decision B: GlowLab Acquisition

**Rahul Sinha (CFO):** Presented the GlowLab acquisition analysis. CFO recommendation: decline at current asking price (5x revenue); revisit post-Series B at market-comparable valuation (1.8–2.2x revenue).

Key points raised:
- Due diligence has revealed a significant misrepresentation: GlowLab's disclosed 29% repeat rate appears to be 90-day registered-to-return, not actual repeat purchases. Actual active 90-day repeat rate is 8.6%.
- One haircare SKU has a BIS compliance gap that GlowLab has not disclosed.
- At NovaSkin's current cash position (INR 3.3 Cr available), an INR 85–95 Cr transaction is structurally impossible without large equity issuance at dilutive terms.

**Pramod Nair (Fireside):** "The misrepresentation on the repeat rate is significant. At 8.6% actual repeat, the customer base isn't worth nearly what they're implying. Walk away. Come back to the conversation if and when their valuation expectations reset."

**Sucharita Bose (Blume):** "I'd add — be careful about how you walk away. These are Blume portfolio companies both, and this is a small world. Decline professionally, leave the door open."

**Dr. Kavya Agarwal:** "What about a haircare white-label option? If the strategic interest is category diversification, NovaSkin can launch haircare SKUs through existing CM relationship for INR 60–80L. That's 0.1% of the acquisition cost for similar strategic outcome."

**CEO:** White-label haircare is noted as an alternative. Will be included in the FY26 product roadmap for consideration.

**Board resolution:** **Management is authorized to formally decline the GlowLab acquisition at current terms.** CFO to communicate decline to GlowLab founders and common investor (Blume) by February 15. White-label haircare alternative to be included in FY26 strategy review.

---

### Decision C: Engineering Team Expansion

**Sneha Rajan (COO):** Presented the tech roadmap and hiring plan. Proposal: hire 10 engineers (immediate plan from Tech Lead) vs. phased 3+4+3 plan over 15 months (COO recommendation).

**Tech Lead Kiran Deshpande (presented briefly):** "The honest answer is we're starting to feel the strain. I'm personally firefighting about 40% of my week, and we don't have the capacity to build any of the things on the roadmap that would meaningfully move revenue. The personalization and mobile app work alone could add INR 4–7 Cr in annualized revenue — but we can't start those projects with the current team."

**Pramod Nair (Fireside):** "The revenue impact numbers are compelling. But the CFO just told us we're within INR 7–9L of a covenant breach if we hire all 10 immediately. That's not a calculated risk — that's a potential crisis trigger. I strongly support the phased plan."

**Sucharita Bose (Blume):** "I agree with the phased approach. I want to add something though: Investor A's diligence specifically flagged tech maturity as a concern. Announcing that NovaSkin is hiring 3 senior engineers right now (not 10, but 3) and showing a roadmap for mobile and personalization — that's actually a positive Series B signal. Frame it right."

**Dr. Kavya Agarwal:** "The attrition risk Kiran mentioned is real. If two junior engineers leave in the next 6 months because there's no growth path, you've got a bigger problem than hiring ahead of revenue. I'd accelerate Phase 1 of the phased plan — hire 3, but do it in Q1 FY25 rather than waiting."

**CEO:** "We'll hire 3 in Q1 FY25 as Phase 1. The Engineering Manager hire is the first priority — Kiran needs management support urgently."

**Board consensus:** **Phased hiring plan approved.** Phase 1 (3 engineers including EM) to be initiated immediately in Q1 FY25. Phase 2 (4 engineers) after Series B close. Phase 3 (3 engineers) contingent on Phase 2 revenue impact validation. **Not a full board resolution — management prerogative, but Board has reviewed and supports.**
