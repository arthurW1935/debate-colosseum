# NovaSkin — Company Profile & Overview
*Internal Document | Last Updated: March 2025*

---

## Company Overview

**Legal name:** NovaSkin Brands Pvt. Ltd.  
**Founded:** 2020, Bengaluru, India  
**Business model:** Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) skincare — primarily online, with selective offline pop-ups  
**Category:** Premium-natural skincare (serums, moisturizers, SPF, cleansers, targeted treatments)  
**Price positioning:** Mid-premium (INR 600–2,800 per SKU, USD 7–34)  
**Website:** novaskin.in | novaskin.co (redirect)  
**Primary sales channels:** Own website (~62%), Amazon India (~21%), Nykaa (~12%), Myntra (~5%)  

---

## Mission & Brand Identity

NovaSkin's stated mission is to make science-backed, dermatologist-validated skincare accessible to urban millennials and Gen Z consumers without the luxury price tag. The brand positions itself between mass-market (Himalaya, Biotique) and luxury-import (The Ordinary UK, Paula's Choice).

Core brand pillars:
- **Ingredient transparency** — full INCI listings, "hero ingredient" callouts on all packaging
- **Dermatologist partnerships** — 3 advisory board dermatologists, formulations co-developed with a Bengaluru-based contract manufacturer (Anthem Cosmeceuticals)
- **Sustainable packaging** — PCR (post-consumer recycled) plastic since Q3 2022, FSC-certified paper inserts
- **Cruelty-free & vegan** — PETA certified since 2021

## Risks & Known Issues

1. **Cash position:** 14-month runway requires Series B close by Q3 2025 — any delay creates operational risk
2. **Minimalist price pressure:** Minimalist's lower price points force NovaSkin to justify premium through efficacy claims and brand trust
3. **Marketplace dependency risk:** Amazon and Nykaa account for 33% of revenue; platform algorithm changes have caused 15–20% GMV swings in past
4. **Key-person risk:** Aryan Mehta (CEO) and Sneha Rajan (COO) hold most institutional knowledge; no formal succession plan
5. **Regulatory:** India's BIS cosmetics regulations tightened in 2024; 3 SKUs under review for labeling compliance
6. **Talent:** Retaining tech and growth marketing talent in Bengaluru is increasingly difficult vs. larger funded competitors
