## Covenant Details — Trifecta Capital Bridge Loan

**Loan amount:** INR 6 Cr  
**Interest rate:** 14% per annum (approx. INR 7L/month)  
**Repayment:** Bullet repayment due August 31, 2025  
**Covenant (EBITDA):** Company must maintain EBITDA ≥ INR 0 on a rolling quarterly basis

**Covenant compliance status:**
- Q3 FY25 (Oct–Dec 2024): EBITDA INR 1.8 Cr ✅
- Q4 FY25 (Jan–Mar 2025): Projected EBITDA INR 0.6 Cr ✅ (thin — Q4 is the slow quarter)
- Q1 FY26 (Apr–Jun 2025): EBITDA forecast INR 0.4 Cr ✅ (very tight)

**Any significant increase in fixed costs risks Q1 FY26 covenant breach.**

**Consequence of covenant breach:**

- Alternatively, they may impose a penalty rate of 18% p.a. and impose additional covenants
- A covenant breach would also complicate the Series B fundraise — investors doing due diligence will see the breach flag

## Seasonal Cash Flow Pattern

NovaSkin's business is highly seasonal. Understanding this is critical for any hiring or investment timing decision:

| Period | Revenue Index | EBITDA Index | Notes |
|--------|--------------|--------------|-------|
| Apr–Jun (Q1) | 75 | 60 | Post-summer; slowest period |
| Jul–Sep (Q2) | 90 | 85 | Recovery; back-to-school, monsoon skincare |
| Oct–Dec (Q3) | 145 | 165 | Peak: Diwali, Christmas, gifting surge |
| Jan–Mar (Q4) | 90 | 90 | Post-peak; decent but not peak |

*Revenue index: 100 = average monthly revenue. Q3 generates approximately 36% of annual revenue.*

**Critical implication:** Any fixed-cost addition (like hiring) hits the hardest in Q1 (Apr–Jun), which is the weakest revenue period. Hiring 10 engineers in Q1 FY25 would coincide with the period of lowest cash generation — worst possible timing.

**Optimal hiring timing:** August–September (before the Q3 revenue surge) to maximize cash runway before new hires are fully productive.
