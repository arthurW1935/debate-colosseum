## Executive Summary

This document presents NovaSkin's three-year financial model across three scenarios: Base Case, Bull Case, and Bear Case. The projections informed the Series B fundraise narrative and are the primary financial reference for strategic investment decisions in FY25.

## Three-Year Projection Model

### Base Case (Most Likely)

*Assumptions: India D2C market grows ~28% CAGR; NovaSkin maintains ~42% YoY; no new geography in FY25; EU expansion piloted in FY26; no large acquisition; hiring stays within plan.*

| | FY25 | FY26 | FY27 |
|-|------|------|------|
| Net Revenue (INR Cr) | 36.0 | 54.2 | 78.6 |
| Gross Margin | 66.1% | 65.5% | 65.0% |
| EBITDA Margin | 6.4% | 7.8% | 11.2% |
| EBITDA (INR Cr) | 2.3 | 4.2 | 8.8 |
| Marketing Spend (% Rev) | 24.7% | 23.1% | 21.0% |
| Headcount | 47 | 62 | 81 |
| CAC (INR) | 820 | 790 | 750 |

### Bull Case (Upside)

*Assumptions: EU pilot exceeds projections (breakeven in 18 months); a Series B of INR 60 Cr closes Q2 FY25; NovaSkin launches SPF and serum range on Amazon UK/DE; aggressive but efficient performance marketing.*

| | FY25 | FY26 | FY27 |
|-|------|------|------|
| Net Revenue (INR Cr) | 39.5 | 68.0 | 115.0 |
| Gross Margin | 66.1% | 64.2% | 63.5% |
| EBITDA Margin | 6.4% | 5.1% | 10.5% |
| EBITDA (INR Cr) | 2.5 | 3.5 | 12.1 |

*EBITDA dip in FY26 due to EU setup costs (regulatory, localization, marketing ramp-up).*

### Bear Case (Downside)

*Assumptions: Series B delayed to Q1 FY26; competition intensifies from Minimalist and new entrants; CAC rises 15%; marketplace algorithm changes cut GMV 10%; no EU expansion.*

| | FY25 | FY26 | FY27 |
|-|------|------|------|
| Net Revenue (INR Cr) | 32.5 | 44.1 | 58.0 |
| Gross Margin | 65.5% | 64.0% | 63.5% |
| EBITDA Margin | 4.1% | 3.8% | 7.5% |
| EBITDA (INR Cr) | 1.3 | 1.7 | 4.4 |

*Bear case does not breach EBITDA covenant but does constrain hiring and R&D investment.*

## Scenario-Specific Financial Impact

### EU Expansion Cost Model (Scenario 1)

*Initial EU pilot targeting UK + Germany, online-only, own website + Amazon EU*

| Cost Item | One-Time (INR Cr) | Annual Recurring (INR Cr) |
|-----------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| EU regulatory (CPNP, SCCS dossiers) | 0.65 | 0.15 (renewal) |
| Product localization (labels, claims) | 0.22 | 0.08 |
| EU warehouse setup (3PL partner) | 0.18 | 0.48 |
| Marketing launch (influencer + Meta EU) | 0.80 | 1.40 |
| Headcount (EU marketing manager + compliance) | — | 0.70 |
| Currency hedging costs | — | 0.12 |
| **Total Year 1 Cost** | **1.85** | **2.93** |

*Break-even on EU channel estimated at 22 months under base case assumptions. EU revenue needed to break even: INR 6.2 Cr/year.*

### Engineer Hiring Cost Model (Scenario 3)

*Proposal: Hire 10 additional engineers ahead of revenue (pre-emptive tech scaling)*

| Role | Count | Annual Cost/Head (INR L) | Total Annual (INR Cr) |
|------|-------|--------------------------|----------------------|
| Senior Backend Engineer | 3 | 28–34 | 0.93 |
| Frontend / App Engineer | 2 | 22–26 | 0.48 |
| Data Engineer | 2 | 26–30 | 0.56 |
| DevOps / Platform | 1 | 30–35 | 0.33 |
| ML / Personalization Engineer | 1 | 35–42 | 0.39 |
| Engineering Manager | 1 | 42–50 | 0.46 |
| **Total** | **10** | — | **3.15** |

*Additional one-time costs: recruiting (INR 12L), onboarding/equipment (INR 8L), desk space expansion (INR 14L) = INR 34L one-time.*

*At current EBITDA of INR 2.3 Cr, adding INR 3.15 Cr in engineering salaries alone would push the company EBITDA-negative by approximately INR 0.85 Cr — a covenant breach on the bridge loan.*
