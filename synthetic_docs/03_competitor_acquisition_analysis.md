# NovaSkin — Acquisition Target Analysis: GlowLab India
*Prepared by: Rahul Sinha (CFO) & Aryan Mehta (CEO)*  
*Date: December 2024 | Classification: Strictly Confidential — Board Eyes Only*

---

## Background

In October 2024, NovaSkin was approached informally by the founders of **GlowLab India** — a Pune-based D2C skincare brand — through a common investor (Blume Ventures). GlowLab's founders indicated interest in a strategic acquisition, citing founder fatigue and investor pressure following two missed growth targets. A preliminary NDA was signed in November 2024.

This document summarizes NovaSkin's analysis of whether to pursue a formal acquisition offer and at what valuation.

---

## GlowLab India — Company Overview

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Founded | 2019, Pune |
| Category | Mid-market skincare + haircare (hybrid) |
| Current Revenue (FY24) | INR 17.8 Cr |
| YoY Growth (FY24) | 11% (down from 38% in FY23) |
| EBITDA Margin | -8.4% (EBITDA-negative; burning ~INR 1.5 Cr/year) |
| Gross Margin | 58.2% (lower than NovaSkin's 66.1%) |
| Headcount | 31 FTE |
| Funding raised | INR 14 Cr (Seed + Series A from Stellaris, Blume) |
| Asking price (informal) | INR 85–95 Cr (approximately 5x FY24 revenue) |
| Active SKUs | 34 (skincare: 19, haircare: 15) |

### GlowLab's Key Assets

**What makes GlowLab interesting:**
- **Offline channel:** 680 retail touchpoints across Modern Trade (Reliance Smart, D-Mart) — NovaSkin has zero offline presence
- **Haircare category:** NovaSkin is pure-skincare; GlowLab's haircare gives immediate category diversification into a INR 12,000 Cr market
- **Pune + West India distribution:** GlowLab is strong in Pune, Mumbai, Ahmedabad — markets where NovaSkin is underindexed
- **CM relationship:** GlowLab manufactures with Wockhardt Pharma's cosmetics division — premium CM with pharmaceutical-grade QC
- **Customer database:** 2.1 lakh registered customers, 29% repeat rate

**What is weak:**
- Revenue growth collapsed from 38% (FY23) to 11% (FY24) — unclear if structural or execution
- EBITDA-negative with no clear path to profitability without significant operational overhaul
- Haircare SKUs have low differentiation; easy substitution by Mamaearth and Wow Skin
- Tech stack is outdated (WooCommerce on shared hosting; no proper CRM or data pipeline)
- Two senior hires left in FY24 (Head of Marketing, Head of Product)

## Valuation Analysis

### GlowLab's Asking Price vs. Market Comparable

| Method | Implied GlowLab Valuation | Implied Multiple |
|--------|--------------------------|-----------------|
| Asking price (founders) | INR 85–95 Cr | 4.8–5.3x FY24 revenue |
| D2C skincare market comps (profitable) | INR 45–55 Cr | 2.5–3.1x revenue |
| D2C skincare market comps (loss-making) | INR 25–35 Cr | 1.4–2.0x revenue |
| DCF model (NovaSkin internal) | INR 28–38 Cr | 1.6–2.1x revenue |

**Assessment:** The founders' asking price of 5x revenue is significantly above market for a loss-making brand with decelerating growth. Comparable transactions in Indian D2C (Mamaearth acqui-hire of B:Blunt at ~2.5x; Honasa acquisition of Dr. Sheth's at ~3.2x for a profitable brand) suggest a fair range of INR 30–45 Cr.

### What NovaSkin Would Actually Pay For

A reverse valuation of GlowLab based on what we would pay for each asset:

| Asset | NovaSkin's Implied Value (INR Cr) |
|-------|----------------------------------|
| Customer database (2.1L customers at INR 400 acquisition cost) | 8.4 |
| Offline distribution channel (680 touchpoints) | 12.0 |
| Haircare SKU portfolio (IP + formulations) | 6.0 |
| Wockhardt CM relationship | 3.5 |
| Headcount (31 FTE, selective retention) | 4.0 |
| Pune warehouse + operations infrastructure | 2.0 |
| Brand name "GlowLab" (moderate recognition in West India) | 3.0 |
| **NovaSkin's total asset-based value** | **38.9** |

*At 5x revenue (~INR 90 Cr), NovaSkin would be overpaying by approximately INR 51 Cr — more than 1.5x NovaSkin's own total funding raised to date.*

## Strategic Case FOR Acquisition

### 1. Category Diversification
Haircare is a natural adjacency. Skincare-haircare cross-sell has a proven playbook (Mamaearth, Plum, Wow). GlowLab's 15 haircare SKUs include two with genuine differentiation (Scalp Serum with Redensyl, Anti-Hairfall Shampoo with Anagain).

### 2. Offline Presence
NovaSkin has no offline channel. GlowLab's 680 MT touchpoints would give immediate presence in organized retail — critical for brand credibility in Tier-1 metros with older demographics who don't shop DTC.

### 3. Speed vs. Build
Building an offline channel from scratch (vendor onboarding, range reviews, planogram negotiations, distribution appointments) would take 18–24 months and cost a similar amount. An acquisition compresses the timeline.

### 4. Talent
GlowLab has a strong supply chain head (Anjali Shah, 8 years at HUL before GlowLab) and a retention-science analyst team that could upgrade NovaSkin's data capabilities.

## Strategic Case AGAINST Acquisition

### 1. NovaSkin Cannot Afford This

At a 5x revenue price (INR 90 Cr), NovaSkin's current cash position (INR 4.2 Cr) is insufficient even for a 5% deposit. The deal would require either:
- Raising Series B specifically for the acquisition (diluting founders significantly)
- An all-equity deal (sharing 30–35% of NovaSkin with GlowLab investors, which our cap table cannot absorb)

**This is a deal-breaker at the asking price without Series B in hand.**

### 2. Integration Risk is Disproportionate

Integrating a 31-person EBITDA-negative company requires management bandwidth NovaSkin does not have. Key risks:
- Culture clash: GlowLab operates on a cost-cutting survival mode; NovaSkin is growth-first
- Tech migration: GlowLab's WooCommerce stack cannot be merged into Shopify Plus; full re-platform needed (INR 1.5–2.0 Cr, 6 months)
- Brand confusion: "NovaSkin" and "GlowLab" are separate brand architectures; running two brands requires duplicated marketing spend

### 3. GlowLab's Deceleration is Unexplained

The drop from 38% to 11% growth in one year is alarming. Due diligence (not yet complete) has not explained this fully. Hypotheses:
- Channel-mix shift: MT shelf rationalization reduced SKU count from 40 to 34
- Founding team fatigue causing underinvestment in marketing in H1 FY24
- Or: structural category saturation in haircare (less favorable hypothesis)

Until we understand the cause, acquiring at 5x revenue carries unquantified downside risk.

### 4. Timing is Wrong

NovaSkin is 14 months from Series B, focused on EBITDA and growth metrics that support a clean fundraise. A large acquisition disrupts both. Post-Series B would be the right time: cash in hand, a larger team to absorb the integration work, and a higher NovaSkin valuation that makes an all-equity deal less dilutive.

## Due Diligence Status

| Area | Status | Finding |
|------|--------|---------|
| Financial statements | Reviewed (FY22–FY24) | Revenue confirmed; EBITDA worse than disclosed initially (-10.2% in H1 FY24) |

| CM agreements | Reviewed | Wockhardt agreement terminable on 90-day notice; not a sticky moat |
| Offline distribution | Site visits (2 of 5 planned) | Shelf presence real but thin; Reliance has flagged underperformance |
| IP / trademarks | Pending legal review | "GlowLab" trademark registered; formulation IP unclear |
| Key employee intent | 2 conversations | Supply chain head (Anjali Shah) open to staying; marketing team likely to leave |
| Regulatory | Pending | One SKU (scalp serum) has undisclosed BIS compliance gap |

**Due diligence red flags so far:**
- Actual active repeat rate of 8.6% vs. disclosed 29% — significant misrepresentation
- BIS compliance gap on a haircare SKU (potential recall risk)
- Wockhardt agreement is weaker than presented (not exclusive, terminable)

## Negotiation Scenarios

### Scenario A — Decline (Current Recommendation)
Do not pursue at 5x revenue. Decline politely; leave door open for revisiting post-Series B at 1.8–2.2x revenue range (INR 32–39 Cr), contingent on GlowLab returning to positive EBITDA.

### Scenario B — Counter-offer at 2x Revenue
Counter at INR 35 Cr (2.0x FY24 revenue), structured as:
- INR 12 Cr cash at close (requires Series B to be in progress)
- INR 23 Cr in NovaSkin equity (4-year earnout tied to GlowLab revenue targets)

**Risk:** GlowLab founders almost certainly decline; wastes relationship capital without bridge. Only pursue if Board directs.

### Scenario C — Asset-only purchase
Negotiate to acquire only the haircare SKU portfolio (IP, formulations, brand right for haircare only) for INR 8–10 Cr cash. Skip the entity, liabilities, offline distribution, and headcount. This is the cleanest deal but GlowLab founders may not accept a partial asset sale.

---

## CFO Recommendation

**Do not proceed with a full acquisition at the current asking price.** The deal as proposed (5x revenue, ~INR 90 Cr) is:
- Financially impossible without a concurrent large fundraise
- Strategically premature given NovaSkin's own runway constraints
- Undermined by due diligence findings (misrepresented repeat rate, compliance gaps)

The CFO strongly recommends **declining the current conversation** and revisiting only after:
1. Series B closes (provides capital buffer)
2. NovaSkin achieves INR 55 Cr+ revenue (makes equity less dilutive)
3. GlowLab's asking price corrects to realistic market comps (2–2.5x revenue)

If the Board wishes to explore haircare adjacency, a **white-label haircare launch** using NovaSkin's existing CM (Anthem) could be executed within the current budget. Estimated cost: INR 60–80L for formulation + launch vs. INR 85–95 Cr acquisition.
