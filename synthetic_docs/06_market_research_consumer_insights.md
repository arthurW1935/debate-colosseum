## 1. India D2C Skincare Market Overview

### Market Size & Growth
- India skincare market (total): INR 18,500 Cr in FY24, growing at ~14% CAGR
- D2C skincare segment: INR 2,800 Cr in FY24, growing at ~28% CAGR (outpacing overall market 2x)
- Percentage of skincare sold online: 34% in FY24 (up from 19% in FY20)
- Projected D2C skincare market by FY27: INR 5,800 Cr

### Key Macro Drivers
1. **Skincare-ification:** Indian consumers, especially women 20–35, are building multi-step skincare routines for the first time — driven by social media, K-beauty influence, and ingredient education
2. **Ingredient literacy:** Google searches for "niacinamide", "retinol", "hyaluronic acid" up 340%, 218%, 189% respectively in India YoY (2023–2024)
3. **Trust deficit with legacy brands:** Survey finding: 68% of surveyed urban millennial women distrust mass-market skincare brands for accurate ingredient labeling
4. **Premiumization:** Average skincare spend per urban woman 22–35 increased from INR 580/month (FY21) to INR 1,240/month (FY24) — a 2.1x increase in 3 years

## 2. NovaSkin Customer Survey — Key Findings

*Survey: 1,240 NovaSkin customers surveyed online, November 2024*

### Why Did You First Purchase NovaSkin?

| Reason | % Respondents |
|--------|--------------|
| Dermatologist/skincare influencer recommendation | 34% |
| Ingredient transparency (knew what was in it) | 28% |
| Friend/family referral | 18% |
| Price point (vs. alternatives) | 12% |
| Packaging / brand aesthetic | 8% |

**Implication:** Word-of-mouth and professional endorsement are the primary acquisition channels — not performance marketing. This explains why NovaSkin's CAC has stayed relatively low despite lower marketing spend vs. peers.

### Satisfaction by Category

| Category | Avg Satisfaction (1–5) |
|----------|----------------------|
| Product efficacy | 4.6 |
| Ingredient transparency | 4.7 |
| Packaging quality | 4.1 |
| Delivery speed | 4.3 |
| Customer support | 4.4 |
| Price-to-value | 4.2 |
| Website experience | 3.6 |
| Mobile app (N/A — no app) | — |

**Critical finding:** Website experience is the weakest-rated dimension at 3.6/5. Qualitative feedback: "hard to find the right product for my skin type," "checkout is clunky on phone." This directly supports the mobile app and personalization roadmap.

### Would You Recommend NovaSkin to Someone?

- NPS: 67 (industry average: 52)
- "Definitely would recommend": 58%
- "Probably would recommend": 31%
- "Wouldn't recommend": 11%

### Barriers to Repeat Purchase

| Barrier | % Citing |
|---------|---------|
| Forgot / no trigger to repurchase | 31% |
| Price | 22% |
| Tried a competitor | 19% |
| Product didn't fully work for me | 17% |
| Couldn't find subscription option | 11% |

**Implication:** "Forgot to repurchase" is the #1 barrier — a retention problem that push notifications (mobile app) and better Klaviyo flows can directly address.

## 4. International Consumer Research

*Survey: 320 respondents in UK and Germany who regularly purchase skincare online, Dec 2024*

### UK Findings

**What brands do you currently use?**
- The Ordinary: 67%
- CeraVe: 58%
- Paula's Choice: 31%
- Medik8: 24%
- NovaSkin: 0% (unprompted awareness)

**After seeing NovaSkin's ingredient list and price (£12–£28), would you consider purchasing?**
- "Definitely yes": 18%
- "Probably yes": 34%
- "Maybe": 27%
- "Probably not": 14%
- "Definitely not": 7%

**What would make you more likely to purchase a new brand like NovaSkin?**
- Reviews from UK skincare influencers I follow: 61%
- Dermatologist recommendation: 54%
- Trial size / lower-risk purchase option: 49%
- Free shipping: 38%
- Money-back guarantee: 35%

### Germany Findings

**What matters most when buying skincare products?**
- Clinical/scientific evidence of efficacy: 71%
- Ingredient transparency: 68%
- Dermatologist endorsement: 64%
- Sustainability of packaging: 59%
- Brand heritage: 42%

**NovaSkin concept interest (Germany):** 47% "probably or definitely" interested after ingredient and positioning explanation.

**Barrier in Germany:** "I don't know this brand, and I'm careful about buying from brands with no track record in Germany" — 63% cited this as a major barrier. **Trust-building through German dermatologist partnerships or clinical study data will be essential.**

## 5. Social Media & Influencer Analysis

### NovaSkin's Current Digital Presence

| Platform | Followers | Avg Engagement Rate | Growth (YoY) |
|----------|-----------|---------------------|--------------|
| Instagram | 512K | 3.8% | +84K |
| YouTube | 89K | 4.2% | +31K |
| TikTok (India) | 234K | 5.9% | +118K |

*Engagement rate of 3.8% on Instagram is strong (industry avg for beauty brands: 2.1–2.8%).*

### Top Performing Content Formats (by conversions attributed)

1. "Before & After" skin transformation posts (Niacinamide, Retinol) — 2.4x average conversion
2. Dermatologist explainer videos (30–60 sec Reels) — 1.9x average conversion
3. Ingredient education carousels ("Why Niacinamide at 10%?") — 1.7x average conversion
4. User-generated content reposts — 1.5x average conversion
5. Discount / sale announcement posts — 1.2x average conversion (strong reach, lower quality traffic)

### Influencer Program Performance

| Tier | Count | Avg Followers | Attribution/INR spend |
|------|-------|---------------|----------------------|
| Mega (>1M) | 3 | 2.1M | 0.8x |
| Macro (200K–1M) | 12 | 450K | 1.4x |
| Mid (50K–200K) | 34 | 98K | 2.1x |
| Micro (<50K) | 89 | 22K | 2.8x |

**Key finding:** Micro-influencers generate 2.8x revenue per INR spent vs. mega-influencers. Yet the macro/mega budget still accounts for 55% of influencer spend. Rebalancing toward micro is an approved H2 FY25 initiative.

## 7. Retention & Cohort Analysis

### 90-Day Repurchase Rate by Acquisition Channel

| Channel | 30-day | 60-day | 90-day |
|---------|--------|--------|--------|
| Organic (SEO/referral) | 8.4% | 19.2% | 32.1% |
| Paid social (Meta) | 5.2% | 13.8% | 24.4% |
| Influencer | 7.1% | 18.3% | 30.7% |
| Amazon (first purchase there) | 3.1% | 8.4% | 14.2% |
| Email (winback) | 11.2% | 24.6% | 38.9% |

**Insight:** Amazon-first customers have dramatically lower retention — they don't get into the NovaSkin email/CRM ecosystem. This is a known problem; the fix involves including a "register your purchase" card in Amazon packaging, which was launched in Q3 FY24 with early signs of improvement.

### 12-Month LTV by Cohort (INR)

| Cohort | 3M LTV | 6M LTV | 12M LTV |
|--------|--------|--------|---------|
| FY22 customers | 620 | 1,340 | 2,140 |
| FY23 customers | 780 | 1,620 | 2,700 |
| FY24 customers (partial) | 940 | 1,890 | 3,100* |

*FY24 12-month LTV is projected based on 6-month actuals + cohort extrapolation.*

**Trend is clear: each new cohort has higher LTV than the previous one** — a strong indicator that the product and brand are improving over time in the eyes of customers.
