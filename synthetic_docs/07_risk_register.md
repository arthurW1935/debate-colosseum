## Category 1: Financial Risks

### FR-01: Series B Fundraise Fails or Delays
- **Description:** NovaSkin's 14-month runway assumes Series B closes by September 2025. A delay of 3–4 months would require bridge financing; a failure to raise at all would trigger restructuring.
- **Likelihood:** 2 (Unlikely — two active term sheet conversations)
- **Impact:** 5 (Catastrophic — existential risk)
- **Risk Score:** 10 (High)
- **Current mitigation:** Two parallel investor conversations; HDFC pre-approved credit line of INR 3 Cr; belt-tightening plan drafted for bear case scenario
- **Residual risk:** Medium — a credit line buys 3 months, not 12
- **Owner:** CEO + CFO

### FR-02: EBITDA Covenant Breach (Trifecta Bridge Loan)
- **Description:** Any month where EBITDA goes negative on a rolling quarterly basis triggers covenant. Specific risks: unexpected large expense, revenue shortfall in Q1, or premature hiring.
- **Likelihood:** 2 (Unlikely under current plan)
- **Impact:** 4 (Significant — forced repayment demand, fundraise complication)
- **Risk Score:** 8 (Medium)
- **Current mitigation:** Monthly EBITDA monitoring; no large fixed-cost additions approved without CFO sign-off; Q1 marketing spend flex plan
- **Owner:** CFO

### FR-03: CAC Inflation
- **Description:** Meta and Google auction dynamics have increased D2C beauty CAC industry-wide by 22% in 18 months. If NovaSkin's CAC rises from INR 820 to INR 1,100+, LTV:CAC falls below 3x.
- **Likelihood:** 3 (Possible)
- **Impact:** 3 (Moderate — margin compression, slower new customer growth)
- **Risk Score:** 9 (Medium)
- **Current mitigation:** Micro-influencer rebalancing (higher ROAS); SEO investment; referral program; email retention reducing dependency on paid acquisition for repeat orders
- **Owner:** Head of Growth

### FR-04: FX Risk (Post-EU Launch)
- **Description:** If NovaSkin begins generating EUR/GBP revenue, a 10% INR appreciation would reduce revenue in INR terms by ~10% for that channel.
- **Likelihood:** 2 (Unlikely in FY25; relevant from FY26)
- **Impact:** 2 (Minor — EU channel is <10% of revenue in first 18 months)
- **Risk Score:** 4 (Low)
- **Current mitigation:** Small forward contracts planned once EU monthly revenue exceeds INR 50L
- **Owner:** CFO

## Category 2: Operational Risks

### OR-01: Single Contract Manufacturer Dependency (Anthem)
- **Description:** 80% of NovaSkin's SKUs are manufactured exclusively by Anthem Cosmeceuticals. A production stoppage (fire, strike, regulatory closure, loss of key personnel) would halt supply for 6–8 weeks minimum.
- **Likelihood:** 2 (Unlikely — Anthem is an established CM with good record)
- **Impact:** 4 (Significant — stockouts across 22 SKUs; estimated INR 3–4 Cr revenue loss per month of stoppage)
- **Risk Score:** 8 (Medium)
- **Current mitigation:** Chemline Industries qualified as secondary CM for 6 SKUs; 45-day inventory buffer
- **Residual risk:** 14 SKUs have no secondary CM qualified — this is a gap
- **Action item:** Qualify Chemline for 8 additional SKUs by Q2 FY25
- **Owner:** Head of Supply Chain

### OR-02: Warehouse / 3PL Failure (Shadowfax)
- **Description:** NovaSkin's fulfillment is almost entirely through Shadowfax Bhiwandi. A warehouse disruption (fire, flood, system failure) or Shadowfax operational issues would halt all dispatch.
- **Likelihood:** 2 (Unlikely)
- **Impact:** 3 (Moderate — 3–5 day disruption manageable; >7 days is revenue-impacting)
- **Risk Score:** 6 (Medium)
- **Current mitigation:** Bengaluru dark store holds 5–7 days of fast-moving SKU inventory
- **Residual risk:** Dark store is not set up for full order dispatch — primarily click-and-collect
- **Owner:** Head of Supply Chain

### OR-03: Shopify Platform Dependency
- **Description:** NovaSkin's entire D2C website runs on Shopify Plus. Any Shopify outage, price increase, or app ecosystem issue directly impacts revenue.
- **Likelihood:** 2 (Unlikely for outage; possible for price/policy change)
- **Impact:** 3 (Moderate — hourly revenue loss of ~INR 25K during peak hours)
- **Risk Score:** 6 (Medium)
- **Current mitigation:** Shopify's uptime SLA is 99.99%; status monitored via Shopify Status + UptimeRobot
- **Owner:** Tech Lead

### OR-04: Inventory Stockout (Hero SKUs)
- **Description:** Demand forecasting errors or supply delays can leave top-selling SKUs (Niacinamide Serum, SPF 50) out of stock, directly losing revenue and damaging rankings.
- **Likelihood:** 3 (Possible — happened 3 times in FY24)
- **Impact:** 3 (Moderate — INR 10–20L per stockout event; Amazon ranking drop can persist 4–6 weeks)
- **Risk Score:** 9 (Medium)
- **Current mitigation:** 45-day buffer for top 5 SKUs; weekly demand review meeting; CM given 90-day rolling forecast
- **Action item:** Real-time inventory sync (tech roadmap item) would reduce this materially
- **Owner:** Head of Supply Chain + Tech Lead

## Category 3: Market & Competitive Risks

### MR-01: Minimalist Price Compression
- **Description:** Minimalist can and does sell comparable formulations at 40–60% of NovaSkin's prices. If consumers become fully price-sensitive, NovaSkin's premium is at risk.
- **Likelihood:** 3 (Possible — already happening at the margin)
- **Impact:** 3 (Moderate — requires brand repositioning investment)
- **Risk Score:** 9 (Medium)
- **Current mitigation:** NovaSkin's positioning on dermatologist validation and LTV metrics suggests price-sensitive customers are not the primary segment; NPS of 67 vs Minimalist's ~58 suggests brand equity is real
- **Owner:** CEO + Head of Growth

### MR-02: Marketplace Algorithm Change
- **Description:** Amazon or Nykaa algorithm changes have previously caused 15–20% GMV swings for NovaSkin in a single month. Increased sponsored ad requirements reduce organic visibility.
- **Likelihood:** 4 (Likely — Amazon algorithm changes are frequent)
- **Impact:** 3 (Moderate — 33% of revenue on marketplaces; a 20% drop is ~INR 6 Cr/year)
- **Risk Score:** 12 (High)
- **Current mitigation:** Increasing own-site revenue mix target (62% → 68% by FY26); building email/app direct relationships
- **Owner:** Head of Growth

### MR-03: New Well-funded Entrant
- **Description:** A well-funded international brand (The Ordinary India expansion, Paula's Choice India deepening) or a new domestic startup could compress NovaSkin's mid-premium segment.
- **Likelihood:** 3 (Possible)
- **Impact:** 3 (Moderate)
- **Risk Score:** 9 (Medium)
- **Current mitigation:** Brand loyalty (NPS 67), dermatologist channel, and LTV metrics provide some protection; continuous product innovation needed
- **Owner:** CEO + Head of Product

## Category 4: Regulatory & Compliance Risks

### RC-01: BIS Cosmetics Regulation Compliance Gap
- **Description:** India's BIS mandatory standards for cosmetics (IS 6608, IS 9325) were updated in 2024. Three of NovaSkin's SKUs are under internal review for labeling compliance. One (Peeling Solution) may need a warning label revision.
- **Likelihood:** 3 (Possible — SKUs are flagged)
- **Impact:** 3 (Moderate — label recall + reprint; possible temporary market withdrawal of affected SKU)
- **Risk Score:** 9 (Medium)
- **Current mitigation:** Legal counsel engaged; SKUs continue to sell pending review; estimated 8-week resolution timeline
- **Owner:** Head of Product + CFO

### RC-02: EU/UK Regulatory Non-Compliance (Pre-Launch)
- **Description:** If NovaSkin launches in EU without completing CPNP notifications or UK CPSR for all pilot SKUs, it would be in breach of EU cosmetics regulation (Regulation EC No 1223/2009).
- **Likelihood:** 2 (Unlikely if regulatory timeline is respected)
- **Impact:** 4 (Significant — product recall, reputational damage, potential fines up to €30,000 per SKU)
- **Risk Score:** 8 (Medium)
- **Current mitigation:** Hard gate: no EU product launch until all CPNP notifications complete; regulatory consultant to be engaged by May 2025
- **Owner:** Head of Product + CFO

### RC-03: Data Privacy (DPDP Act India)
- **Description:** India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) 2023 rules were finalized in late 2024. NovaSkin collects significant personal data (skin concerns, purchase history, location) via website and CRM.
- **Likelihood:** 3 (Possible — many D2C brands are non-compliant in small ways)
- **Impact:** 3 (Moderate — fines up to INR 250 Cr for major breaches; reputational damage)
- **Risk Score:** 9 (Medium)
- **Current mitigation:** Privacy policy updated Nov 2024; consent flows updated on website; CRM data audit in progress
- **Owner:** CFO + Tech Lead

## Category 6: EU Expansion-Specific Risks

*(These risks only become active if EU expansion is approved)*

### EU-01: AHA Formula Non-Compliance
- **Description:** NovaSkin's AHA 30% + BHA 2% Peeling Solution exceeds EU OTC cosmetic limits (max 10% AHA at pH < 3.5). Selling this SKU in EU without formula modification would be illegal.
- **Likelihood:** 5 (Confirmed — this is a known issue)
- **Impact:** 3 (Moderate — SKU must be excluded or reformulated)
- **Risk Score:** 15 (High)
- **Mitigation:** Exclude from EU SKU list (confirmed action)
- **Owner:** Head of Product

### EU-02: Responsible Person Non-Setup
- **Description:** UK and EU both require a locally-based Responsible Person before any products can be sold. Without this, all sales are illegal.
- **Likelihood:** 2 (Unlikely if timeline respected)
- **Impact:** 4 (Significant — no EU revenue is possible without this)
- **Risk Score:** 8 (Medium)
- **Mitigation:** Hard gate on regulatory setup completion before any EU sales
- **Owner:** CFO + Head of Product

### EU-03: Brand Awareness Zero-State
- **Description:** NovaSkin has no brand recognition in UK or Germany. Initial EU CAC will be substantially higher than India — estimated 2.5–3.5x India CAC until brand awareness builds.
- **Likelihood:** 5 (Certain — no EU marketing has been done)
- **Impact:** 2 (Minor — priced into EU financial model; break-even assumes high initial CAC)
- **Risk Score:** 10 (High)
- **Mitigation:** Diaspora-first targeting reduces cold-audience CAC; influencer seeding before paid media launch
- **Owner:** Head of Growth
